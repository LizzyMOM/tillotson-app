How it works

  • Each sheet lists only the drivers it applies to: Option 1 for karts, toolbox renters for toolboxes, and so on.
  • Returns only accept items that were handed out, and show what that driver took.
  • Lead: if a driver needs more weight, open their lead record and tap Add more lead. Each top-up is counted, charged and logged, and the return checks against the full total.
  • Missing tools and missing lead are priced from the organiser's list and charged by card, cash or from the €300 deposit.
  • Organisers can add a sheet for any other item and choose who gets it, whether it comes back and what it costs.
  • Prices for tools and lead are placeholders until the organiser sets them.
